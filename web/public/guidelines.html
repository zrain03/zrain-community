ZRAIN COMMUNITY

Community guidelines.

Constructive feedback makes products better for everyone.

Be respectful

Discuss products and features, not people's private lives. Do not post threats, harassment, hateful content, impersonations, spam, scams or illegal material.

Protect privacy

Do not post passwords, private API keys, financial details, addresses, phone numbers, or personal information about others.

Keep it relevant

Each product has its own conversation space. Choose the right product and topic. Use the Report button if content violates these guidelines.

Moderation

Comments that pass automated checks are published immediately. Zrain may hide or permanently delete comments and replies that violate these guidelines and may act on reports. Automated checks are not a guarantee that all unsafe content is detected. Feedback critical of a product is welcome when constructive.

Need help?

For a privacy or moderation concern, contact zrain.dev@gmail.com. Please include the product name and comment ID if available, not any secret key.
