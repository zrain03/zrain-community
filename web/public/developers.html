Security & privacy

Comments are moderated, rate limited, and grouped by an immutable product ID. External developer access is approved only after independent website ownership verification. A developer key can moderate only projects assigned to its owner.

Developer keys are secrets. Keep them in server-side environments or in the private developer dashboard, never in source code, URL parameters, screenshots, or public repositories. For problems, email zrain.dev@gmail.com.
